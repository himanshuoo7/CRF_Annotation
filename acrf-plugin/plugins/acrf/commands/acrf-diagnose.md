description: Explain why an annotated CRF has fewer annotations than expected, or why pages came out NOT SUBMITTED.
---

Follow the **Diagnosing a thin result** section of the `acrf` skill.

Work the causes in pipeline order — an early failure explains every later
symptom, so do not report four problems when there is one. Pages that carry no
submitted data are supposed to say NOT SUBMITTED.
